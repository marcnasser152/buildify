Fig. 06 — How a build runs

Four stages. No black box.

Every build, regardless of discipline, moves through the same four stages — so you always know what's happening and when it ships.

01

Spec

We sit down — in person or on a call — and turn your idea into a real spec: what it does, who uses it, and what "done" actually looks like.

02

Build

We build in the open. You see progress as it happens, not a status update once a month and a surprise at the end.

03

Launch

We ship it, connect it to what you already use, and make sure it holds up under real users — not just a demo.

04

Support

We stay on the build after launch — fixes, updates, and the next version whenever you're ready for it.
