Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 24, 2026

If you have interacted with Buildify through Instagram, our website, or any connected service, and you would like your personal data deleted from our systems, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request that we delete your personal information, please email us directly at:

info@buildifyplatform.com

In your email, please include:

  1. Your name and/or the Instagram username you used to contact us
  2. A brief description of your request (e.g., "Please delete all data associated with my account")
  3. Any additional information that may help us locate your records

What Happens Next

Once we receive your request, we will:

  1. Verify your identity to the extent necessary to protect your data
  2. Locate and remove your personal information from our active systems and records, including any customer relationship management (CRM) records
  3. Confirm completion of the deletion via email, typically within 30 days

What Data May Be Deleted

This may include information such as your name, contact details, Instagram username or user ID, message history, and any notes associated with your record in our systems.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us at info@buildifyplatform.com.
